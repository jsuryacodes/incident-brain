## What this shows

- At 2,000 services and 9,375 dependencies every tool answers well under FalkorDB's 1 s timeout. The slowest are `shared_dependency_changes` (163.9 ms) and `blast_radius` (136.8 ms): they enumerate every path so the UI can draw them.
- The whole-graph algorithms (PageRank, betweenness, WCC, label propagation) take at most 6.1 ms on 2,000 services: ranking a company's services is a single call, not an offline job.
- Honest note: for plain reachability, algo.BFS (0.6 ms) and variable-length Cypher (0.6 ms) are a tie at this size; the algorithms earn their place by answering different questions in one call (which services everything depends on, which alerts cannot share a cause, which communities exist), not by being faster at reachability.
